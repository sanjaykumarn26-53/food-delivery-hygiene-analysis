# Food Delivery Safety & Hygiene Perception Analysis

Survey-based analysis of how customers perceive hygiene, packaging and delivery safety in food delivery services.

## Overview
A 20-question survey was collected from 138 respondents. Data was cleaned in Excel and analyzed in Python.

## Tools and methods
- Tools: Python, Excel, Google Forms
- Methods: Regression, Chi-Square test, One-Way ANOVA, Correlation

## Key finding
Packaging quality and sealed delivery emerged as the strongest drivers of customer confidence in food safety.
